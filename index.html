HOW TO PLAY

CONTROLS

Stick (left) — push to dive, pull to climb, left/right to bank into a turn.

Power (right) — engine throttle. Keep your speed up or the wing will stall.

Guns / Bombs — fire the twin Vickers; pickle bombs on ground targets.

View (left slider) — slide between chase distances; the bottom is the cockpit.

THE SORTIE

Follow the pulsing beacons, the edge arrows, and the top-right radar to your objectives. Strafe nests, bomb bunkers, and dogfight Fokkers.

Watch the HULL bar and your FUEL. Hitting the ground is a crash. Don't fly off the map — the flak will find you.

NEED IT EASIER?

Turn on Flight Assist, Aim Assist, Reinforced Hull or Unlimited Fuel in Options. New pilots should start with Flight School.
